নাম শিখি Privacy Policy

How নাম শিখি handles information when you use the app.

Learn offline.

নাম শিখি does not collect or transmit personal information to ITHub. Favorites and viewed-item progress stay on your device.

Effective date: 26 November 2026
Developer: ITHub
Android app: নাম শিখি (Names; bd.com.ithub.names)

About this policy

This privacy policy applies to নাম শিখি (Names), the Android learning app developed by ITHub. It teaches Bangla and English names through pictures, bundled pronunciations, favorites, and quizzes. The app works offline and does not require an account.

Information used on your device

The app saves the identifiers of your favorite and viewed items in its private storage to remember your collection and learning progress. Searches, quiz answers, and scores are processed locally. Android may temporarily retain the current screen, search text, and quiz state to restore an interrupted session. None of this information is sent to ITHub or a third party by the app.

Pictures, learning content, and pronunciation audio are included with the app. Audio playback uses your device’s media volume and audio focus; the app does not record your voice or access the microphone.

Permissions and data collection

The current version requests no Android permissions, including no internet, location, camera, microphone, or contacts permission. It does not read your photos or files outside its private app storage.

The app has no registration or login, advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics, tracking SDKs, or third-party crash reporting SDKs. It does not collect, sell, or share personal information, device identifiers, or usage statistics with ITHub or other parties.

External links and platform services

Some image credits contain links to Wikimedia Commons or Creative Commons. If you choose to open one, your browser connects to that website, whose privacy policy applies. The app itself does not send your favorites, learning progress, searches, or quiz answers to those websites.

Android, Google Play, and your device manufacturer may separately process information under their own settings and privacy policies, such as installation, updates, and optional system diagnostics. This policy describes the app’s own data handling.

Storage, security, and backups

Favorites and viewed-item progress are stored in Android’s private app storage, protected by Android’s application sandbox. The app is configured to disable Android app backups and exclude its data from cloud backup and device transfer. ITHub does not maintain a server-side database of app users or their learning activity.

Retention and deleting your data

Favorites and viewed-item progress remain on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. You can also remove individual favorites within the app. To reset all saved information, open Android Settings → Apps → নাম শিখি → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage or Clear data. Labels vary by device. Clearing storage resets your collection and progress but does not remove the learning content bundled with the app.

Uninstalling the app removes its private app data. There is no account or server-side profile to delete, and ITHub cannot retrieve information stored only on your device.

Children’s privacy

নাম শিখি is an educational app that may be used by children. It does not collect personal information from any user, including children, and has no accounts, advertising, or tracking. Parents and guardians can delete locally saved information using the steps above.

Contacting ITHub or visiting this website

If you choose to email ITHub, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that correspondence to respond and handle your request, and retain it only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence by emailing us. This voluntary communication is separate from information stored inside নাম শিখি.

When you open this policy or another page on our website, our hosting provider may process connection information to deliver and protect the page. Website browsing and contact services are described in the ITHub website privacy notice.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when নাম শিখি’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will identify the latest revision. Any future data collection will be described in an updated policy and accompanied by any required in-app disclosures or consent.

Privacy questions and requests

For questions about নাম শিখি or this privacy policy, contact ITHub at ithub.com.bd@gmail.com. Please mention নাম শিখি (Names) in your message.
