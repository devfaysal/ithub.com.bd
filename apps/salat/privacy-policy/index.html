Salat Privacy Policy

How Salat handles information when you use the app.

Prayer times, offline.

Salat does not collect or transmit personal data to ITHub. Your district and calculation preferences stay on your device.

Effective date: 23 November 2026
Developer: ITHub
Android app: Salat (bd.com.ithub.salat)

About this policy

This privacy policy applies to the Salat Android app developed by ITHub, displayed on Android as সালাতের সময় (Salat Time). Salat displays prayer times for districts in Bangladesh using a timetable included with the app. It works without an internet connection and does not require an account.

Information used on your device

Salat stores your selected district, prayer calculation method, and Asr calculation preference in the app’s private storage so it can remember your choices. These selections can reflect your location or religious preferences; they are used only on your device and are not sent to ITHub or any third party by the app.

You choose a district manually. Salat does not detect or track your physical location. District searches and the date you view are handled locally; the app does not send search terms or a viewing history to a server.

The app reads device settings such as the 12/24-hour clock format and light or dark appearance to display its interface. It uses the current date and time to show the timetable and highlight the next prayer. This processing happens on your device.

Permissions and data collection

The current version of Salat requests no Android permissions, including no internet or location permission. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files outside its private app storage.

Salat has no registration or login, advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics, tracking SDKs, or third-party crash reporting SDKs. The app does not collect or transmit personal information, usage statistics, or device identifiers to ITHub.

Prayer timetable and third parties

The prayer timetable is bundled with the app and was compiled from Aladhan calendar data during development. Salat does not connect to Aladhan or send your district or preferences to that service when you use the app. The app does not sell or share your locally stored information.

Android, Google Play, and your device manufacturer may separately process information under their own settings and privacy policies, for example when you install or update an app or choose to share system diagnostics. This policy describes Salat’s own data handling.

Storage, security, and backups

Preferences and the working copy of the prayer timetable are stored in Android’s private app storage, protected by Android’s application sandbox. Salat is configured to disable Android app backups and exclude its app data from cloud backup and device transfer. The app does not maintain a server-side database of users or their preferences.

Retention and deleting your data

Your saved preferences remain on your device until you change them, clear Salat’s app storage, or uninstall the app. To remove them, open Android Settings, find Salat under Apps, open Storage (or Storage & cache), and choose Clear storage or Clear data. The exact labels vary by device. Clearing storage resets your preferences; the bundled prayer timetable is recreated when you next open the app.

Uninstalling Salat removes its private app data. There is no Salat account or server-side app profile to delete, and ITHub cannot retrieve preferences stored only on your device.

Children’s privacy

Salat does not collect personal information from any user, including children. It has no accounts, advertising, or tracking features.

Contacting ITHub or visiting this website

If you choose to email ITHub, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that correspondence to respond and handle your request, and retain it only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence by emailing us. This voluntary communication is separate from information stored inside Salat.

When you open this policy or another page on our website, our hosting provider may process connection information to deliver and protect the page. Website browsing and contact services are described in the ITHub website privacy notice.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Salat’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will identify the latest revision. Any future data collection will be described in an updated policy and accompanied by any required in-app disclosures or consent.

Privacy questions and requests

For questions about Salat or this privacy policy, contact ITHub at ithub.com.bd@gmail.com. Please mention Salat in your message.
