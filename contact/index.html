Before we talk

A few useful answers.

Can you help improve an existing application?

Yes. Share the current system and the problems you want to solve. We’ll review what’s there and discuss a sensible scope for maintenance, integrations, or modernisation.

Do I need to know exactly how AI will fit?

No. Start with a task that takes too much time or information that’s difficult to use. We can explore whether AI is useful for that task and what a focused pilot could look like.

How are projects priced?

We discuss the goals, scope, and timeline first, then prepare a proposal. The cost depends on the work involved and the support you need.

Can I ask about one of your products?

Of course. Choose the product in the enquiry form or contact us directly. CampusControl is in early access; Masjid Management and Amar Pirijpur are in development.
