privacy notice.

How this website handles information when you browse or contact us.

Browsing the website

This website does not use advertising trackers or marketing analytics. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may process routine connection information such as IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect the website.

Using the enquiry form

When you send the contact form, your name, email address, organisation (if supplied), topic, and message are sent to ITHub through Cloudflare and our email delivery provider, Resend. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and discuss related work. The website does not maintain a separate enquiry database. Messages are delivered to our inbox and are also processed by our email providers.

Spam protection

The contact page uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help prevent automated submissions. Turnstile processes technical browser and connection information for verification. This protection is separate from advertising analytics.

Email and WhatsApp

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to share and use it to respond, discuss your request, and manage related work. Your email provider or WhatsApp also processes communications under its own policies. Please avoid including passwords or other sensitive credentials in an initial enquiry.

Product websites

External product websites and services operate separately and may have their own accounts, forms, and privacy notices. Follow their notices when using those services.

Questions or requests

For questions about information you have shared with ITHub, email ithub.com.bd@gmail.com.
