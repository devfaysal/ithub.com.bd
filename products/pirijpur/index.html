Amar Pirijpur

Local knowledge.
Closer connections.

A community directory for Pirijpur, Bajitpur—an initiative by ITHub.

Community initiative

Find what you need.
Closer to home.

Amar Pirijpur is being built to help people find local shops, healthcare providers, educational institutions, technicians, and other essential services in one place.

With a Bangla interface and a focus on local information, the initiative aims to make useful connections easier for the community.
